About this prototype

  • Prototype. Lake Dive is an early, experimental part of the Lake Sentry website. It may change, contain mistakes, or be switched off at any time.
  • Not a real lake. The lake, the waste, the fish and the robot are drawn by computer code. It does not show any specific lake or the current condition of any lake.
  • Imitating real conditions. It loosely imitates conditions reported at urban lakes in and around Hyderabad, such as floating and sunken plastic. Amounts, colours and depths are illustrative and not to scale.
  • The robot. The Lake Sentry robot shown is an artist's impression of our V1 prototype.
  • Not advice. This is an awareness piece, not scientific, legal, health or safety advice.
  • Corrections. Spotted something wrong? Tell us through the contact form.
