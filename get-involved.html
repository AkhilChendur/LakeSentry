Get involved

“Lake pollution is a problem.” So what can we do about it?

Most people already know lakes are polluted. Lake Sentry is about turning that awareness into action. Here's how you can help.

Step one

Take the Lake Sentry survey

The survey asks what floating waste you've seen, what you think causes it, how serious it is and whether you'd support technology to clean it up. Every response makes the research stronger and shapes what we build next.

Scan the QR code with your phone's camera to open the survey.

A direct link for desktop visitors will be added here soon.

What people told us

Survey highlights (40 respondents)

  • Point to littering and poor waste disposal as the main cause87.5%
  • Would support an innovative bot to clean lakes80%
  • Believe people are aware of the dangers but don't act on them63%

Feedback we're taking seriously

Scope & feasibility

Could this extend to oceans? And a low-cost design matters: the benefits have to outweigh the cost for governments to adopt it.

Enforcement & accountability

Several people felt the deeper issue is weak enforcement and indifference. Even official bodies sometimes dispose of waste improperly.

Test carefully first

People want rigorous testing, including making sure the robot can't harm fish or other wildlife.

Encouragement

Many respondents were simply glad someone is working on this, and shared tips for making the survey easier to read.

Working together

Are you an NGO, a lake authority or a CSR team?

Lake Sentry wants to join existing lake-restoration efforts, not duplicate them. We are looking for partners to help with site access and permissions, supervised test runs, training volunteer operators and, once the model is proven, funding to build more robots.
