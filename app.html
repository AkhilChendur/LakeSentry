Tool 1

Explore Hyderabad's lakes

Hover over a lake to see its name. Select it, with a click or by pressing Enter, to open its lake page. You can also use the list.

All lakes

    Tool 2

    Drive Lake Sentry V1

    Pilot the remote-controlled robot around a lake. Drive forward into trash so the mesh conveyor lifts it aboard, then return to the yellow zone on the bank to unload and record your trip. Try the original smooth conveyor rod to see the slipping problem V1 had to solve.

    1. 1 · Aim Steer toward trash
    2. 2 · Scoop Conveyor lifts it
    3. 3 · Return Head to the bank
    4. 4 · Record Weigh & log

    Collect floating trash, then bring it back to the bank.

    Controls: click the lake or tab to it, then use ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D. Press P to pause. On a touchscreen, hold the buttons below.
