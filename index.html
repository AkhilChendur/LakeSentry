The dual-solution framework

Cleaning the waste already in our lakes is necessary. Stopping new waste from getting there matters just as much.

  • 01 · Prevent

    Awareness campaign

    Instagram, LinkedIn and offline installations such as “The Rollers” that encourage people to dispose of waste properly.

  • 02 · Remove

    Lake Sentry V1 robot

    A remote-controlled floating machine built from CPVC pipe that scoops trash off the surface using a mesh conveyor belt.

  • 03 · Connect

    LakeSentry.in platform

    A lake map, a research page for each lake, and a reviewed network of community clean-up initiatives.
