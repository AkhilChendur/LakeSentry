Get involved

“Lake pollution is a problem.” So what can we do about it?

Most people already know lakes are polluted. Lake Sentry is about turning that awareness into action. Here's how you can help.

Step one

Take the Lake Sentry survey

The survey asks what floating waste you've noticed, what you think causes lake pollution, how serious it is, whether you'd support technology to clean it up, and how much awareness versus individual action matters. Every response makes the research stronger and shapes what gets built next.

Feedback we're taking seriously

Scope & feasibility

Could this extend to oceans? A low-cost design matters: the benefits have to outweigh the cost for governments to adopt it.

Enforcement & accountability

Several people felt the deeper issue is weak enforcement and indifference. Even official bodies sometimes dispose of waste improperly.

Test carefully first

People want rigorous testing, including making sure the bot can't harm fish or other wildlife, plus robust data to answer sceptics.

Encouragement

Many respondents were simply glad someone is working on this, and shared tips for making the survey easier to read.
